# Play the Myhra preview

This preview contains the rebuilt Myhra hole, saved rounds, separate practice and manual real-round replay. Holes 2–18 remain the earlier course preview. The clubhouse and range follow the Myhra milestone.

## First round

1. Open the Vercel preview in the owner's signed-in Chrome session. Preview protection is enabled; another browser/device may need Vercel sign-in. The current deployment link is in the acceptance report.
2. Tap the small caddie recommendation to apply its club, line and suggested strength. Open its chevron for the explanation. Aim with a ground tap/drag or the left/right controls. A two-finger gesture moves the camera.
3. On touch, pull down inside the circular swing area until the displayed power matches your intended strength, then release. Moving into the marked side cancellation area cancels. The same 96 CSS-pixel pull reaches full power in portrait or landscape.
4. On the green, choose the labelled 6, 15 or 35 m putting range before the gesture. The grid follows the playing surface; moving indicators show downhill direction. The projected ground path shows assisted break. A light pull gives fine short-putt control.

The full predicted shot includes carry, landing and rollout. An imperfect stroke can differ from that nominal line; forgiving strike assistance is visible in settings. Accepted shots and scores save before animation, so reloading during flight cannot add a second stroke. If browser storage is full, the game rejects the shot and keeps the previous round; free some storage and retry.

## Mouse, keyboard and controller

- Mouse: pull back in the swing area, then deliberately move forward through the starting point. Releasing a backed-up mouse does not strike.
- Keyboard: A/D or left/right aim; up/down change target strength; C changes club; R resets the camera; V scouts the hole. Space operates timing input. In settings, select three-click for start → power → accuracy.
- Standard controller: left stick aims/sets target power, shoulder buttons change clubs, right stick pulls back then deliberately pushes through. Returning to neutral does not strike. Cross operates timing input, Square scouts, Options opens settings, Circle cancels. Neutral-rearm is required after connection/interruption. The actual DualSense hardware result remains untested.
- Cart: use the circular touch stick, WASD/arrows or left stick. Hold Shift/R2, or toggle Boost, for controlled faster travel. Release to brake; reverse uses S/down or the opposite stick direction. Back to my ball returns to golf.

## Practice and remembered rounds

Practice is in Menu. It keeps your unfinished round separate; use Return to saved round when finished. Settings include daylight, passing time, wind, prediction/grid visibility and graphics tiers.

Open the book icon for the round journal. Enter an 18-hole scorecard, leaving unknown date, tee, pars or scores blank. For Myhra, place remembered shot endpoints on the overhead map and record penalties separately. Correct any score/shot discrepancy shown by the journal. Remembered positions are approximate; the connecting replay paths are illustrative.

Use play/pause, scrub or shot selection to revisit an entered hole. Try this shot differently creates a separate scenario with changed club, aim or conditions. It preserves the recorded original. A total of 77 by itself is a benchmark and contains no invented hole scores or shots.

Records are local to each browser origin. Export versioned JSON from the journal for a backup or another preview/device; importing creates copies and preserves originals. Accounts and launch-monitor/GPS providers are outside this milestone.

## Review before release

Try the tee shot, an approach, a short and long putt, cancellation, cart steering/braking and return from practice. Review golfer grip/contact from several camera angles and use the included motion footage. Physical iPhone, actual DualSense, coach review, independent bounce/roll/cup measurements and club reference approval remain acceptance gates; see `MYHRA_REBUILD_ACCEPTANCE.md`.
