MYHRA ASSET ATTRIBUTION

Terrain: existing Grenland Kartverket-derived source; see parent course manifest.
Routing: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Tree atlas, sky HDR and grass / sand / gravel textures: Poly Haven, CC0.
https://polyhaven.com/license
Asset-specific links and transformations are recorded in sources.json.

Golfer, cart and birch: generated in the project owner's Tripo account.
These are generated assets, not CC0 Poly Haven assets. Retopologized and
compressed for this game. Club meshes and runtime IK animation are authored
project code. No Mixamo motion-capture downloads are included.

Grenland course diagrams and drone imagery were reviewed as references only.
No club photographs or videos are redistributed in this directory.
