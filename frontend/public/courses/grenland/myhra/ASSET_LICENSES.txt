MYHRA ASSET ATTRIBUTION

Terrain: existing Grenland Kartverket-derived source; see parent course manifest.
Routing: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Tree atlas, sky HDR and grass / sand / gravel textures: Poly Haven, CC0.
https://polyhaven.com/license
Asset-specific links and transformations are recorded in sources.json.

Golfer, cart and birch: generated in the project owner's Tripo account.
These are generated assets, not CC0 Poly Haven assets. Retopologized and
compressed for this game. Club meshes and runtime IK animation are authored
project code. The embedded golfer actions now use owner-downloaded Mixamo
golf body motions with project-authored grip, club and terrain corrections.

Grenland course diagrams and drone imagery were reviewed as references only.
No club photographs or videos are redistributed in this directory.

REBUILD DERIVATIVES (5 October 2026)

Near fir meshes and mobile HDR remain Poly Haven CC0 derivatives.
Mobile golfer/cart/birch variants retain their generated-source rights.
The logged-in project account showed Pro; Tripo paid-user modification and
commercial distribution guidance was reviewed at:
https://www.tripo3d.ai/help/privacy-policy/how-to-use-tripo-models-commercially

GolfGrip was authored in Blender. GolfDriver/GolfIron/GolfChip/GolfPutt use
Adobe Mixamo golf FBX motions downloaded by the owner, retargeted and trimmed
in Blender, with corrected avatar proportions and anatomical hand frames.
The authored club calibration and grip constraints accompany those motions.
Mixamo permits embedded video-game use; no raw Mixamo FBX or new working
Blend file is distributed in this repository or as an animation asset pack.
Retarget build script and trim/provenance metadata are included. Earlier
project-authored Blender actions remain as historical fallback sources.
https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html
https://community.adobe.com/questions-696/mixamo-faq-licensing-royalties-ownership-eula-and-tos-589400
Professional technique remains subject to coach review. New Tripo spend: 0.

height datasets retained. See sources.json and hole.json surfaceAuthorship.
